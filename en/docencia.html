Teaching

I teach semiotics, audiovisual communication and radio in UTPL's Communication degree, and supervise undergraduate and postgraduate theses. Since 2019 most of my teaching innovation projects have tested sound and visual formats and, more recently, artificial intelligence.

Thesis supervision

I have supervised 30 undergraduate and master's theses at UTPL on digital narratives, audiovisual communication and media. The theses are available in the institutional repository.
