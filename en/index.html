Ph.D. in Communication from the University of Huelva (Spain, 2021, cum laude). Master's degrees in Educational Management and Leadership (UTPL, 2014) and in Communication, Ideation and Content Creation with Artificial Intelligence (UTPL, 2026).

He has been a tenured full-time lecturer at UTPL since 2019. Before that he taught for eleven years at the Pontificia Universidad Católica del Ecuador, Santo Domingo campus (2008–2019), where he directed the Social Communication programme from 2013 to 2015.

His research deals with communication and audiovisual culture: the image and its meaning, new audiovisual and sound narratives, and the role of artificial intelligence in producing and teaching communication. He belongs to the research groups Communication and Audiovisual Culture (GICA) and Educational Technology Innovation (GITEINNOEDUCA), and reviews for national and international scholarly journals.

What I research

Research

  • Image, sign and meaning

    A semiotic reading of the visual forms that circulate in media culture: memes, photography, urban landscape and national symbols. The underlying question is how an image comes to mean something to a community.

  • Audiovisual and sound narratives

    Stories on digital platforms, online radio and podcasts, with attention to orality and heritage: how legends, traditions and urban experience find new formats.

  • Artificial intelligence and communication

    Uses of AI in sound narrative and content production, and the ethical dilemmas it opens for journalism: disinformation, authorship and the governance of digital narratives.

  • Media literacy and journalism

    Audiovisual competences of adolescents and communication students, communicative citizenship and journalistic quality in crisis coverage.
