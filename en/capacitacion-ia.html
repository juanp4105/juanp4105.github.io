Training in artificial intelligence

I design and teach training for educators who want to use artificial intelligence with judgement: knowing what it does well, where it fails and how to make it support better teaching. Every course ends in concrete outputs that participants take back to their classrooms.

  1. From chalk to code: education evolves with AI
  2. Artificial intelligence tools for teaching and creative production
  3. Creating teaching resources with AI

Workshop

From chalk to code: education evolves with AI

For
Primary and secondary school teachers
Length
10 hours, theory and practice
Format
In person, groups of 30 to 40 teachers

Topics

  • Conversational assistants and subject-specific educational chatbots
  • AI-assisted planning and idea organisation: maps, diagrams and documents
  • Creating images, music, video and avatars for teaching resources
  • Transcribing and summarising sessions
  • Ethical AI use at school: AI empowers teachers, it does not replace them

Course

Artificial intelligence tools for teaching and creative production

For
Teachers and students interested in content production
Length
Three modules, 2026 edition
Format
In person or online

Topics

  • Pedagogical foundations: the probabilistic nature of AI and algorithmic opacity
  • Ethics, bias, authorship and academic integrity; checking against sources
  • Prompt structure: role, context, information, purpose and format
  • Educational chatbots by subject and level
  • AI for research and visual organisation of ideas
  • Audiovisual production: script, voice, music, image and editing

Workshop

Creating teaching resources with AI

For
Upper-secondary and university teachers
Length
Flexible: one intensive day or a modular course
Format
In person or online

Topics

  • From learning goal to prompt: plan the resource before generating it
  • Written materials: study guides, cases, rubrics and question banks
  • Visual resources: infographics, diagrams and illustrations
  • Audio and video: synthetic-voice narration, podcasts and avatar video
  • Publishing in the virtual classroom (Moodle, Canvas, D2L)
  • Fact-checking, copyright and transparency about AI use
